About

I'm a computational geographer working at the intersection of hydrology, remote sensing, and public health — currently a GIS Analyst at SPEKTER GmbH in Bavaria, Germany, where I build coupled 1D/2D flood models for municipal stormwater and river systems.

Alongside that, I'm a Research Assistant at the Space and Environment Research Centre (SERC) and an MS candidate in GIS for Environment and Development at Jahangirnagar University. My work spans wetland methane dynamics in Bangladesh's Haor basin, dengue risk mapping for Chattogram City Corporation, and forest phytosociology in Lawachara National Park — tying satellite data and field measurement to the environmental and health outcomes they actually drive.

Publications

2025
Decades of Transformation: Quantifying the Effects of Urbanisation on the Hydroclimate of Dhaka, Bangladesh
Saha, G., Rafi, Roy, B. · Under Review

LULC change detection (1980–2023) via Google Earth Engine combined with multivariate regression on climate records; finds Dhaka's built-up area expanded more than 7× since 1980, with a warming trend of ~0.21°C per decade linked to urban heat island intensification.

2025
Vector-Borne Infectious Diseases of Chattogram City: A Geo-Spatial Investigation on Dengue-Infected Patients and Breeding Sites of Aedes Mosquitoes
Roy, B., Barua, E., Koley, N. J. (Corresponding Author) · Pre-print

Ward-level spatial epidemiology using a 16-parameter AHP model and Poisson regression; identified six very-high-risk wards, with low-elevation, high-density wards accounting for 68% of cases on 35% of land area. Risk maps adopted by Chattogram City Corporation for vector control.
